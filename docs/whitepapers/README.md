# Industry whitepapers

These documents develop industry, product and business hypotheses from Personal AgentOS design work. They are **non-normative research/editorial artifacts**, not implementation contracts or statements that proposed capabilities have shipped.

## Whose Agent?

**누구의 에이전트인가 — AI 공급자 중심의 통합 환경에서 사용자 주도형 Agent 생태계로**

- [Read the Korean manuscript — integrated review draft v0.2](whose-agent.ko.md).
- [Editorial provenance, reviewer personas and ten-round review plan](review-plan.en.md).
- [Publication issue — WHITEPAPER-01 #1043](https://github.com/Jongtae/agentos/issues/1043).

The manuscript combines the original industry/business argument, the Jarvis opening thought experiment, and a chapter on verifiable execution and portable evidence. It covers the internet/mobile analogy, portable memory, replaceable intelligence, persistent Presence, human-agent collaboration, skills, evidence exchange, business incentives, counterarguments and an evaluation agenda.

**Review status:** a working draft. Ten review/rewrite rounds and final editor/verifier approvals have not been completed. The review-plan document describes roles and gates; it does not claim that autonomous or independent reviewer agents have run. The PR is for review, not an approved release.

Korean is the source language of this owner-facing manuscript. This directory guide and editorial process are in English. No English translation of the manuscript is claimed. Canonical development guidance remains in [AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md), the [Development Constitution](../development-constitution.en.md), and the relevant English product contracts. Nothing here changes `delivery-plan.yaml`, runtime authority, the active program, or README product claims.

The case-study source is the already-public [2026-09-23 Presence and Settings UX research](../research/presence-and-settings-ux.ko.md). Historical observations, industry inferences, hypothetical examples and proposed experiments must remain distinguishable. Public references are collected in the manuscript's bibliography.
