## Review personas

These are role briefs, not identities or qualifications of actual participants. When work is assigned, record the actual reviewer/tool, observed model setting only when available, and whether the reviewer was independent of the author.

| Persona | Responsibility | Must not do |
|---|---|---|
| Developmental editor | Preserve the owner's industrial thesis, audience, argument flow and depth; remove repetition without narrowing the scope | Substitute a generic agent survey or feature backlog for the owner's argument |
| Industry-history reviewer | Test the network/mobile analogy, distinguish architectural separation from power/profit migration, and examine contrary examples | Treat an analogy or one historical market as a universal forecast |
| Agent architecture reviewer | Examine model/runtime/context/authority separation, continuity and interoperability boundaries | Present a proposal as shipped capability or require new task-specific core code |
| HCI and collaboration reviewer | Review Presence, intervention, repair, shared work state and expectation calibration | Equate anthropomorphic language, more notifications or higher trust with better outcomes |
| Security and evidence reviewer | Test actor attribution, evidence semantics, incompleteness, signatures, receipts, replay and privacy limits | Claim a blockchain, signature or service assertion makes its content true |
| Business-model reviewer | Examine buyer, payer, incentives, unit economics, maintenance and counterarguments | Invent market sizes, customer demand or validated willingness to pay |
| Final editor | Decide whether this exact manuscript is coherent, useful, readable and faithful enough to publish as a vision/strategy whitepaper | Approve a different revision, waive factual blockers, or certify technical truth by style judgment |
| Final verifier | Decide whether this exact manuscript's factual claims, source scope and evidence labels are supportable | Treat author self-review as independent validation or certify unperformed experiments |

## Final approval gates

**Final editor — PENDING**

The editor must confirm that the opening leads to the actual thesis; the historical frame has limits; memory, intelligence, Presence, collaboration, evidence and business chapters form one argument; terminology is consistent; counterarguments are substantive; proposals are actionable but not disguised forecasts; and the Korean manuscript preserves the owner's intent without raw operational chatter.

**Final verifier — PENDING**

The verifier must confirm that every load-bearing fact has an appropriate source and period; hypothetical examples are labeled; reported project observations are not upgraded into fresh measurements; signed/registered evidence is not confused with truth or completeness; actor attribution does not identify an unproven human; source limitations and unresolved factual blockers are explicit; and no unperformed test/review/approval is represented as completed.

Both gates must reference the **same exact manuscript commit or blob digest**, the supporting source set, findings and dispositions. Approval attaches to that reviewed artifact, not to a persona name, an unchanged claim of satisfaction, or an unrelated repository head. Any later substantive manuscript/source change requires the affected gate to be reconsidered. An accepted limitation may remain in a published vision paper; a known false factual claim may not.

GitHub approval and editorial approval are separate. Do not manufacture GitHub reviews under invented users. The publication PR must remain a draft until the maintainer chooses otherwise; no automatic merge is authorized by this issue.

## Record format for actual rounds

For each round record:

- round ID, date, exact input revision and assigned role;
- actual reviewer/tool, observed execution and independence/self-review status;
- finding ID, severity, exact location, source or rationale, required change;
- resulting revision or no-change justification;
- unresolved findings and disposition;
- the final editor/verifier decision where applicable.

Do not include private chain-of-thought, secret prompts, credentials or personal data. A concise findings/revision audit is sufficient.
