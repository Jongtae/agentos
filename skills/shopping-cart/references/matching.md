# Matching the exact product

Compare these before any change:

1. **Product:** the item the owner named, including the brand if they named one.
2. **Variant:** flavor, fat level, organic or antibiotic-free, size grade. If the owner gave none, prefer the plain or standard one and say which you chose.
3. **Pack size and unit:** volume or weight per item, and items per pack. 1L and 900ml×2 are different products.
4. **Seller and shop:** stay in the shop the owner named. Do not move to another seller or mall section unless the owner asked.
5. **Availability:** out of stock, or a "delivery not available" note for the owner's delivery type, means you cannot add it now.

Saved owner preferences (memory) can break a tie. They never override what the owner said in this request.

If two candidates still fit after these checks, ask once, listing each with its pack size and price.
