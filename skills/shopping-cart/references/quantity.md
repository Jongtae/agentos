# What a quantity in the request means

Three requests look alike but differ:

| The owner says | Meaning | With 1 already in the cart |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "우유 하나 더 담아줘", "add one more milk" | **Add** N to whatever is there | 2 |
| "우유 2개로 해줘", "make it 2 milks" | **Set** the total to N | 2 (change the quantity, not a new line) |
| "우유 담아줘", "put milk in the cart" | **Ensure** at least N, usually 1 | 1, so nothing to do. Say it is already there |

- "Ensure" is the reading for a plain request with no "더/more" and no total.
- If the owner clearly wants a different reading, follow them.
- A pack is not a unit: "우유 2개" of a 2-pack means two packs unless the owner says otherwise.
- When the shop only offers a quantity control on the cart line, set that line's quantity rather than adding a second line.
